## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & SCOPE
Act as a Principal Full-Stack Mobile & Web Architect and Design Systems Engineer. Your objective is to build **STUDYNUT**, a world-class, high-converting learning application tailored strictly for students in **Grades 6th to 10th (Key Stage 3 / IB MYP up to Cambridge IGCSE Stage 11)**.

The app must be responsive, maintain zero layout shift, and run seamlessly across **Android, iOS, and Web** using **Flutter** or **React Native with Expo (Tailwind CSS / NativeWind)**.

---

## 2. AUTOMATED DATA PIPELINE & CLOUD ENGINE

The app relies on an automated backend pipeline to scrape, parse, format, and deliver live 2026 Cambridge/IB syllabus materials and PDF resources:

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        STUDYNUT AUTOMATED PIPELINE                     │
│                                                                        │
│  [1. Python Scraper Bot]  ──►  [2. AI Vision & Parser]  ──►  [3. Cloud Storage]
│  - Automated Cron Job        - Extracts questions & diagrams  - AWS S3 / Cloudflare CDN
│  - Scrapes Board Portals     - Formats math to KaTeX          - PostgreSQL Database
│  - Downloads Syllabus PDFs   - Tags Grade, Year & Difficulty  - Local Offline Caching
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘


1. **Web Scraper Engine (Python / Playwright / BeautifulSoup):**
   * Automatically monitors official Cambridge portals (`cambridgeinternational.org`) via scheduled cron jobs to detect and download new 2026 past papers, mark schemes, specimen papers, and syllabus PDFs.
2. **AI Vision & Text Parser (GPT-4o Vision / Claude 3.5 Sonnet API):**
   * Processes raw PDF pages and extracts questions, diagrams, and marking keys.
   * Auto-formats complex mathematical expressions and chemical formulas into clean **KaTeX**.
   * Structures questions into JSON tagged with **Stage/Grade**, **Subject**, **Paper Code** (e.g., Paper 2 MCQ / Paper 4 Extended), **Chapter**, **Difficulty Tier** (Easy, Medium, Hard), and **Step-by-Step Mark Scheme Rationale**.
3. **Cloud Storage & CDN (AWS S3 / Cloudflare R2):**
   * Caches all downloaded PDFs, mark schemes, mind maps, and formula sheets on a global CDN for <100ms load times.
   * Implements local encrypted caching (using SQLite/Hive) for **100% Offline Access**.

## 5. MODULE & FEATURE SPECIFICATIONS

### 1. Stage & Syllabus Selector Engine
* Persistent switcher between **Stage 7 to 9 (Lower Sec)** and **Stage 10 to 11 (IGCSE / IB MYP)** with automatic subject-split reconfiguration.

### 2. Digital Textbook & Live PDF Engine
* Embedded in-app PDF reader (`flutter_pdfview` / `pdfjs`) featuring pinch-to-zoom, page jump, highlighting, annotation, and bookmarking.
* Direct CDN integration with official **2026 Cambridge Specimen PDFs and Formula Booklets**.

### 3. Practice Engine (Past Papers / PYQs & MCQs)
* **Topical MCQ Bank:** Filterable by 3 difficulty levels (**Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard**).
* **Previous Year Questions (PYQs):** Past board paper bank with official mark schemes and step-by-step solutions.
* **Formula & Equation Engine:** Full mathematical and chemical equation rendering via **KaTeX**.

### 4. Interactive Learning Artifacts
* **Smart Audio / Read-Aloud Notes:** Audio narration player integrated into chapter revision summaries.
* **Visual Mind Maps:** Interactive pinch-to-zoom SVG flowcharts for complex topics (e.g., IGCSE Chemistry Organic Reactions or Physics Waves).
* **3D Flip Flashcards:** Interactive cards for quick recall of definitions, key terms, and historical dates.
* **KaTeX Formula Sheets:** Reference sheets for Physics equations, Math theorems, and Periodic Tables.

### 5. Exam Simulator & Custom Test Creator
* **School Exam Simulator:** Preset timed mock exams matching real board formats:
  * **Cambridge Paper 2 (MCQ - 45 Mins)**
  * **Cambridge Paper 4 (Extended Theory - 2 Hours)**
* **Custom Test Generator:** Allows students to build custom tests by selecting Stage, Subject, Chapters, Time Limit, and Difficulty Mix.

### 6. NutAI Multimodal Vision Doubt Solver & AI Tutor
* **Vision Assistant (Camera & Image Upload):**
  * OCR + Image understanding tool: Accepts photos, PDF docs, or direct camera uploads of textbook diagrams and past paper questions.
  * Generates instant step-by-step solutions formatted in **KaTeX** using Cambridge mark scheme guidelines.
* **NutAI Conversational Tutor:** Interactive assistant to explain concepts, guide through command terms (*Evaluate, Explain, Calculate*), and give real-world analogies.

### 7. Offline Download Manager & PDF Caching
* Secure local storage engine allowing students to save textbooks, past papers, mark schemes, and mind maps onto their phone storage for offline study.

### 8. Gamification & Study Analytics
* **Daily Streak System:** Flame indicator with streak freeze protection.
* **Goal Tracker:** Set daily goals for study minutes and questions solved.
* **Bookmarks & Revision Vault:** Automatically saves weak/incorrect questions into a custom revision folder.

1. **Frontend:** Flutter (Dart) OR React Native (TypeScript + Expo) with Tailwind CSS / NativeWind.
