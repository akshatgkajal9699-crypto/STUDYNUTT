## 7. MONETIZATION, SUBSCRIPTION TIERS & PAYWALL ARCHITECTURE

### A. Subscription Pricing Matrix & Billing Logic

STUDYNUT operates on a Freemium subscription model designed to maximize acquisition while driving high conversion through high-value AI evaluation, voice examination, and Cambridge-specific exam simulation features.

* **Free Tier (Acquisition Hook):** Unlimited access to standard notes, formula sheets, and basic MCQs. Restricted access to hero conversion features to demonstrate value.
* **PRO Subscriptions (Unrestricted Access):**
  * **Monthly Plan:** `$3.99 / month` (Short-term exam crash course)
  * **6-Month Plan:** `$17.99 / 6 months` (~$2.99/month — 25% savings)
  * **Yearly Plan:** `$29.99 / year` (~$2.49/month — 37% best value savings)
  * **Trial Period:** 7-Day Free Trial offered on 6-Month and Yearly plans with App Store / Play Store / Stripe billing integration.

#### 1. Reverse Mark Scheme Engine (Handwritten Paper Grader & OCR)
* **Free Tier:** 1 free handwritten paper evaluation per week.
* **PRO Tier:** Unlimited scans.
* **Execution Logic:**
  * Student snaps a photo of handwritten work on physical paper.
  * Backend processes image using Multimodal Vision OCR and evaluates against official Cambridge mark schemes.
  * Overlays digital visual feedback (`+1 Method Mark`, `-1 Accuracy Mark`, `Missing Units Alert`) directly onto the student's handwritten image.

#### 2. Digital Paper 2 & 4 CBT Simulator (Cambridge On-Screen Mode)
* **Free Tier:** Standard untimed practice quizzes.
* **PRO Tier:** Unlocks full **Cambridge On-Screen Exam Simulator**.
* **Execution Logic:**
  * Replicates Cambridge's official digital assessment interface: side-by-side question viewer, strike-through option eliminators, flag-for-review tools, on-screen non-calculator scratchpad, and countdown timer.
  * Generates post-exam analytics tracking time spent per question and pacing bottlenecks.

#### 3. Command Term Keyword Highlighter
* **Free Tier:** Shows basic static definitions for words like *State* or *Describe*.
* **PRO Tier:** Unlocks real-time contextual **Command Term Rubric Cards** across all Paper 4 structured theory questions.
* **Execution Logic:**
  * Highlights keywords (*Evaluate, Calculate, Explain, Suggest, Describe*) in gold text.
  * Tapping the word displays Cambridge mark-scheme rules explaining how many points are allocated and common mistakes that result in lost marks.

#### 4. Socratic Ghost Mode (Real-Time Voice AI Examiner)
* **Free Tier:** 1 free session per month (capped at 60 seconds).
* **PRO Tier:** Unlimited full-length viva oral sessions.
* **Execution Logic:**
  * Uses WebRTC / Real-Time Voice API to act as an official Cambridge oral examiner.
  * Asks rapid-fire conceptual questions after a student completes an IGCSE topic.
  * Listens to the student's spoken response and evaluates it against Cambridge command term rubrics (*Describe, Explain, Evaluate*) with instant verbal feedback.

### C. Paywall UI & Trigger Architecture

The app MUST trigger a high-converting Glassmorphic Paywall Modal in the following scenarios:

1. **Quota Exceeded Trigger:** When a free user attempts their 2nd handwritten paper scan in a single week.
2. **CBT Mode Trigger:** When a free user taps "Launch On-Screen Exam Mode" inside any past paper.
3. **Command Card Trigger:** When a free user taps an advanced command term rubric card.
4. **Ghost Mode Trigger:** When a free user attempts a 2nd voice examination session or exceeds the 60-second trial cap.
